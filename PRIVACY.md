# PokeBrawl – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy has two parts: the PokeBrawl Discord bot (Part 1) and the optional, anonymous performance reports in the game (Part 2).

## Part 1: The PokeBrawl Discord bot

This policy covers the PokeBrawl bot (application ID 1567822915561332797), which runs only on the official PokeBrawl Discord server. PokeBrawl is a free, non-commercial fan project.

### What the bot stores

- **Your Discord user ID**, together with your XP, your level and how many messages you have written (the level system). The level system never stores the text of your messages, only the fact that a message was sent.
- **Ticket transcripts**: when a support ticket is closed, a transcript of that ticket channel is posted to a staff-only log channel, so the team can follow up on support cases.
- **Removed invite links**: messages are checked automatically for invites to other Discord servers. Such a message is deleted, and a staff-only log channel gets your user ID, username, the channel, the time and the removed text, so the team can act on spam.
- **Giveaway entries** are read from the giveaway channel while a giveaway runs and are not stored elsewhere.
- **Player reports from the game**: when a logged-in player reports another player in the game, our account server keeps the report: both account IDs and usernames, the reason, the optional short note, the match (mode, stage, match ID), both teams, the result and the last chat lines of that match as the reporter's game saw them. No IP address and no e-mail are stored with it. The bot posts each report to a private staff channel that only the PokeBrawl owner and admins can see.
- **Fair-play checks in online matches**: so the PokeBrawl team can review possible cheating, the game may send technical match data to our account server (both account IDs, the match and what looked wrong) and, in some cases, a short clip of that match. No IP address and no e-mail are stored with it. Nothing happens automatically to an account: the team looks at it first.
- **Protecting accounts and fair play**: when you create an account or log in, our account server keeps a scrambled code made from your IP address, and when you create an account also one made from your device (the IP address and the device ID themselves are never stored or sent). They are used only to stop banned players from making new accounts and to limit sign-ups (one new account per device a week). At most the last 5 codes are kept per account, each for 30 days, and they are deleted at once if you delete your account.
- **Match confirmation**: our game servers tell the account server which account took part in which online match, when, and how much game data was exchanged (no IP address, no chat), so that results only count for matches that really happened. This is kept for 3 days.
- **Evidence clip with a report**: during an online match the game keeps the last 2 minutes of that match's game data (the fighters' positions, moves and battle events; no chat, no voice, no IP address) in memory only. It is never saved to your device and never sent anywhere, unless you report a player from that match (within 10 minutes): then it is sent once together with the report. The account server keeps it next to the report and deletes it with the report after 90 days (or at once if you delete your account). Only the PokeBrawl owner and admins can watch it, and every viewing is logged.

### What the bot uses but does not store

- **Your username, display name, avatar and the server's member count** are used once to draw the welcome card when you join. The card is posted in the welcome channel and the data is not kept anywhere else.
- **Your roles** are read and changed for join roles, reaction roles (e.g. language roles) and level roles.
- **Voice channel state** is used to create and remove temporary voice rooms.

### What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. We do not use the data for advertising or tracking. Message texts are only checked automatically for server invite links; nothing else in them is read or stored.

### Where the data lives

The data is stored on our own server (Europe) and is accessible only to the PokeBrawl team.

### How long we keep it

Level data is kept while the server exists, so your level stays if you come back. Ticket transcripts stay in the staff log until they are no longer needed. Player reports are deleted from the account server after 90 days; reports you filed are deleted at once if you delete your account. The copy in the staff channel stays until the team removes it. Fair-play check data is deleted after 30 days. The IP codes are deleted after 30 days, match confirmations after 3 days.

### Your rights

You can ask us to show or delete your data at any time. Open a ticket on the PokeBrawl Discord server and we will handle it within 30 days. When we delete your level data, your level roles are removed too.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.

## Part 2: Anonymous performance reports in the game (optional)

**Off unless you say yes.** Since version 0.16.0 (November 2026), the game asks you once whether you want to
help us make PokeBrawl run better on devices like yours. You can change your answer at any time in
**Options > Game > Share performance data**. Please only say yes if you are 16 or older, or if a
parent agrees.

**What is sent if you say yes.** After each battle, one short summary:

- how smoothly the battle ran: frames per second, slow frames, stutters, freezes;
- the type of device: platform and system version, the phone or tablet model (on PCs and Macs only
  the processor family, never a model or machine name), graphics chip, memory and screen size as
  ranges;
- the game: version, graphics settings, stage, game mode, number of fighters, battle length as a
  range, and for online battles the average ping;
- whether the battle used the lighter stage graphics we are testing on phones (yes / no).

To test such a change on half of the devices first, a game that shares performance data keeps a random
number (0-99) on the device that decides which half it is in. It is never sent and is deleted when you
switch sharing off. At start, every game also downloads a small list of these test switches from our
server; nothing about you or your device is sent with it.

**What is never sent or stored:** your name, account, e-mail, friends, IP address, any device or
installation ID, language, location, time of day, or anything you type.

**Where it goes and for how long.** Only to our own server in the European Union, into a database
of its own that is not part of any backup and is never shared, sold or used for advertising. The
server notes only the day a report arrived and deletes every report after **90 days**. To deliver a
report your device connects to the server, which sees your IP address for that connection but never
saves it.

**Why, and on what basis.** We use the reports only to find out on which devices PokeBrawl runs badly
and whether an update made it better or worse. Reading this information from your device and sending
it needs your consent (§ 25 (1) German TDDDG); your "yes" is that consent, and you can withdraw it
at any time. The reports are designed not to contain personal data.

**Your choices.** Switch it off in Options > Game: from then on nothing is sent, and reports still
waiting on your device are deleted at once. Reports already sent contain nothing that identifies you,
so we cannot find or show "your" reports; they are deleted automatically after 90 days.

**Copy performance report** (Options > Game) only copies a text about your last battles to your
clipboard, for you to paste into Discord yourself. It sends nothing.

Questions: open a ticket on the PokeBrawl Discord server.
