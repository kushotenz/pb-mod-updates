# PokeBrawl Discord Bot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the PokeBrawl bot (application ID 1567822915561332797), which runs only on the official PokeBrawl Discord server. PokeBrawl is a free, non-commercial fan project.

## What the bot stores

- **Your Discord user ID**, together with your XP, your level and how many messages you have written (the level system). The text of your messages is never read or stored, only the fact that a message was sent.
- **Ticket transcripts**: when a support ticket is closed, a transcript of that ticket channel is posted to a staff-only log channel, so the team can follow up on support cases.
- **Giveaway entries** are read from the giveaway channel while a giveaway runs and are not stored elsewhere.

## What the bot uses but does not store

- **Your username, display name, avatar and the server's member count** are used once to draw the welcome card when you join. The card is posted in the welcome channel and the data is not kept anywhere else.
- **Your roles** are read and changed for join roles, reaction roles (e.g. language roles) and level roles.
- **Voice channel state** is used to create and remove temporary voice rooms.

## What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. We do not use the data for advertising or tracking. We do not read the content of your messages.

## Where the data lives

The data is stored on our own server (Europe) and is accessible only to the PokeBrawl team.

## How long we keep it

Level data is kept while the server exists, so your level stays if you come back. Ticket transcripts stay in the staff log until they are no longer needed.

## Your rights

You can ask us to show or delete your data at any time. Open a ticket on the PokeBrawl Discord server and we will handle it within 30 days. When we delete your level data, your level roles are removed too.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
